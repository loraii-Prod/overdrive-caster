# Staff pick'em (internal)

The casters and staff predict every Major match: the winner and the series score. It is separate from the public
Pick'em and from the desk picks shown on air.

- **Producer:** Studio → Graphics & Data → **Staff pick'em**. Copy the private link and send it to the staff. From
  here you also edit the staff list, see everyone's picks, lock a match by hand (or keep it open after its start time
  if the match is delayed), remove a pick, reset all picks, and see the leaderboard. **New link** replaces the link;
  the old one stops working at once.
- **Staff:** open the link on a phone or PC, tap your name (the device remembers it), then for each open match tap
  the winner and the score (3-0 / 3-1 / 3-2). The pick saves when both are chosen and can be changed until the match
  starts.
- **Locking:** a match locks at its start time, at its first map score, when a result is entered, or when the
  producer locks it. Before it locks, staff see only their own picks and who has picked. After that, everyone's picks
  are visible.
- **Points:** 1 for the right winner, 3 for the right winner and the exact score. Ties are broken by exact scores.
- **Staff list:** Boney, GGeek, Legion2k, NuaLaoch, Padster, The_Toble, Turbo, Orion, Max, Babz. Edit it in Studio.
  Removing a name keeps that person's picks; adding it back shows them again.
- **Trust:** anyone with the link can pick under any name on the list. It is an internal page, so share the link
  only with staff, and replace it if it leaks.
- Saved with the event, like the rest of the show.
